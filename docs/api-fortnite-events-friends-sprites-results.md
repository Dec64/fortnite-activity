# api-fortnite.com Pro: events, friends and sprites

Evidence captured 15–16 September 2026. This is API research only. All saved responses are sanitized; private calls used an in-memory Epic access token after the exact account-identity check. The request ledger is [`probe-summary.json`](../evidence/sanitized/api-fortnite/probe-summary.json). It records 81 dispatched requests across the investigation: 66 GET and 15 device-auth or interactive-auth POST requests. One cashprizes request was interrupted during local sanitization, so its exact HTTP status and timestamps are unknown; a later captured repeat returned 200. No account-changing, friend/party, purchase, publish, upload or replay-parsing operation was called.

## Sprite collection

The first 200 responses for `sprites/versions`, `sprites` and `sprites/collection/all` were projected as empty summaries because their payloads are inside `data`. A synthetic-tested, bounded wrapper projection and one repeat of each route recovered the data. An explicit `version=42.10` call to the own-collection endpoint recovered current family and variant details. The earlier two unversioned own-collection 200 responses still have no saved body shape; their status alone remains evidence of route access.

| Observed route | Result |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /api/v2/sprites/versions` | 200; four versions: 42.10 current, 42.00, 41.30 and 41.20 |
| `GET /api/v2/sprites` | 200; version 42.10 catalogue, 16 families, ten level-up curve entries, one event and 11 spawn lists |
| `GET /api/v2/sprites/collection/all` | 200; four versions, cumulative 26/179 variants and 18/41 families, 14.53% as returned by the provider |
| `GET /api/v2/sprites/collection?version=42.10` | 200; 23/61 current variants, 15/16 families, 37.7%, seven mastered variant records; all 16 families and 61 variant ownership records captured |

The current version includes a missing Klombo Sprite family. The four per-version totals are 23/61, 17/36, 3/118 and 1/97. They overlap: adding them would produce 44 owned variant records, while the provider's cross-version deduplicated total is 26. Keep current and cumulative percentages separate. Family/variant names and safe ownership/mastery fields are in the [current sanitized collection](../evidence/sanitized/api-fortnite/20260916T080056Z/sprites_collection_current.json); no image URL or personal identifier was saved. The [version](../evidence/sanitized/api-fortnite/20260916T075859Z/sprites_versions.json), [catalogue](../evidence/sanitized/api-fortnite/20260916T075859Z/sprites_catalog.json) and [all-versions](../evidence/sanitized/api-fortnite/20260916T075859Z/sprites_collection_all.json) summaries preserve source version and timestamp.

## Tournament data

`GET /api/v1/events/global?lang=en` returned 200 with 24 top-level entries. The captured safe records include FNCS, Reload and Solo Victory Cup groups, their regions, platforms and time windows. `GET /api/v1/events/player?region=EU&accountId={accountId}` returned 200 with 40 event records and 165 resolved window locations, plus scoring, payout and template structures. These are event data made available for the queried player, not proof of registration or eligibility. The player `scores` array was empty. [Global evidence](../evidence/sanitized/api-fortnite/20260915T192435Z/events_global.json) and [EU player evidence](../evidence/sanitized/api-fortnite/20260915T192801Z/events_player.json) support a useful tournament schedule and rule display with source timestamps. Global extraction was capped at 500 nested records for safety, so it is not a complete flattened event export.

`GET /api/v1/events/cashprizes` and `/api/v1/events/scoring` returned 200 with 4,420 and 4,875 top-level keyed entries respectively. The first cashprizes request was interrupted in local processing and has no preserved status; the later captured request is the evidence for 200. A sample upcoming EU Solo Victory Cup showed why window-specific rules matter:

| Window | Observed scoring | Observed payout shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `S42_SoloVictoryCup_Event3Round1_EU` | Placement rule with 50 tiers; team eliminations give two points per elimination | Rank-threshold 4,000 grants a token; this is a qualification reward, not a cash prize |
| `S42_SoloVictoryCup_Event3Round2_EU` | One placement rule, first place 100 points | Three `ecomm` USD payout records with quantities 100, 200 and 300 and thresholds 100, 200 and 300 |

The final-round table's `scoringType=value` and thresholds need additional semantic verification before translating them into placement or payout amounts in a user-facing view. Do not promise any particular cash amount from this structural response alone. [Qualifier scoring](../evidence/sanitized/api-fortnite/20260916T080056Z/events_scoring_solo_detail.json), [qualifier reward](../evidence/sanitized/api-fortnite/20260916T080056Z/events_cashprize_solo_detail.json), [final scoring](../evidence/sanitized/api-fortnite/20260916T080206Z/events_scoring_solo_final.json) and [final reward/currency](../evidence/sanitized/api-fortnite/20260916T080324Z/events_cashprize_solo_final_currency.json) are saved.

`GET /api/v1/events/tracker/eligibility/{identifier}/{eventId}` returned 200 for the Solo Victory Cup's upcoming EU qualifier. It said `isEligible=true`, with two verified `mustNotHave` requirements met, **and six unverified requirements**: account level, Secure Boot, TPM 2.0, IOMMU, minimum iOS version and 2FA. Its `estimatedCurrentLevel=164197` is not reconciled with the profile-level endpoint's different account-level figure; do not display it as a trusted account level. `isEligible=true` therefore means the provider's checked subset passed, not that Player One is confirmed able to enter. Official rules, age requirements and applicable platform/security checks remain separate. See [sanitized eligibility evidence](../evidence/sanitized/api-fortnite/20260916T075859Z/events_eligibility_solo.json).

`GET /api/v1/events/tracker` returned 200 with an empty `tournaments_played` array and `valid_for_tracker_count=0`; that is not a general ineligibility finding. `GET /api/v1/events/tokens` returned 200 with one account entry, but the token fields were intentionally omitted from the generic safe projection. Personal power rankings returned 200 and numeric rank/points fields; the meaning and period of that rank need validation before display. No dedicated official English broadcast URL was found in the provider specification. A `canLiveSpectate` flag in event windows is not a broadcast link.

The later [SDK-led history check](api-fortnite-python-sdk-audit.md) used the current Pro endpoint `GET /api/v1/events/tracker/eligibility?days=180&requiredTournaments=14` with Player One's verified token. Its first response had `history.complete=false`, 4,202/4,210 windows resolved and `eligible=null`; the bounded repeat completed 4,210/4,210 and returned 0/14, `eligible=false`. This establishes that **the provider's specific 14-tournaments-in-180-days criterion is not met**. It does not say the upcoming Solo Victory Cup requires that criterion, or settle that event's six unverified requirements. [First response](../evidence/sanitized/api-fortnite/20260916T081843Z/events_tracker_history_completeness.json), [completed response](../evidence/sanitized/api-fortnite/20260916T082110Z/events_tracker_history_completion_retry.json).

The paginated global leaderboard for a completed Solo Victory Cup window returned 200: 100 entries on page 0, 40 reported pages, score/rank/point breakdowns, session-history shapes and an update timestamp. The sample had no `liveSessions`. This supports a tournament standings view, subject to pagination and event/window mapping; it is not live player telemetry. [Leaderboard evidence](../evidence/sanitized/api-fortnite/20260916T080852Z/events_leaderboard_solo_past.json) omits account IDs and display names. Player One's Pro recent tournament-matches route returned 200 with `tournamentCount=0`, `totalMatches=0` in its documented roughly 36-hour window. A separate v2 participation-history route returned 404; its error text was suppressed, so the 404's cause is not established. Neither result proves that he has never competed. [Recent matches](../evidence/sanitized/api-fortnite/20260916T080852Z/events_recent_matches.json) and [history failure](../evidence/sanitized/api-fortnite/20260916T080852Z/events_history_v2.json) are saved.

## Friends and current session

Both Friends summary and full-list routes returned 403 using the same exact-identity token that succeeded on player events. A second summary call returned 403 with an error classified as permission-related. The OpenAPI advertises both routes for Pro, so the observed failure is an access/scope discrepancy, not evidence of zero friends. No friend identity or presence data was obtained. Do not infer the second player's identity or activity.

`GET /api/v1/events/player/{accountId}/session` returned 403 twice. The second sanitized response explicitly had `required=[custom]` and `current=[pro]`; the current paid Pro tier cannot use this route. Its documented description concerns a current match/party signal with backend lag, but it is not a usable Pro presence source here. [Friends diagnosis](../evidence/sanitized/api-fortnite/20260916T080206Z/friends_summary_diagnosis.json) and [session tier diagnosis](../evidence/sanitized/api-fortnite/20260916T080206Z/events_player_session_diagnosis.json) preserve the endpoint-specific outcomes without credential or error-text leakage.

The provider now clearly covers sprite ownership and much of the tournament schedule/rule data. It still does not supply a working Pro presence route, official English broadcasts, complete participation/age eligibility, or the second player's separately authorized private data.

## Current free-offer check

`GET /api/v1/shop?lang=en` returned 200. The bounded projection inspected 287 catalogue entries across 36 storefronts and found zero entries with a numeric `finalPrice=0` in the returned price arrays. No usable shop expiration timestamp was returned in the captured summary. This is a point-in-time Item Shop finding, not proof that the game has no free cosmetics or quest rewards. The shop does not expose Player One's ownership, personal eligibility, quest target or reward deadline.
